# Brag Plan: Blue-tech

## What is this app?
A full-stack laptop store for Rwanda ("Considered objects"), with a curated catalog, a USD/RWF price toggle, and checkout by card, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, all running on Supabase.

## The angle
Blue-tech sells laptops "chosen with care, not just specs", and the video is made with the same restraint: a quiet premium product film, no hype. The specific hook is the last mile. Most stores stop at "add to cart". Blue-tech shows the price in RWF, then a mobile-money prompt landing on a phone, then "Order Confirmed". The MoMo flow is the real product moment, so the video ends there.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen, then "Considered objects." (the site's own H1) sets in large Space Grotesk, word by word, with the tagline "Built to last — laptops chosen with care, not just specs." fading in underneath. It earns the next 20 seconds by promising taste, not features.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The four real promo banners from the homepage carousel arrive one by one as cards, each in its own brand gradient: "Built for the frame rate.", "Work that keeps up with you.", "Back to class, sorted.", "Trade up, not down."
- The currency toggle: a price card flips from `$1,000` to `1,471,000 RWF` (the real fallback rate in the code is 1471). The number counts up.
- The MoMo checkout: cursor selects MTN Mobile Money, a phone number types in, and "A payment prompt has been sent to 0788 000 000" appears with a phone approving it.

## Outro / punchline
Green "Order Confirmed" lands on a strong beat, then wipes to the Blue-tech mark, "Considered objects." and a quiet payment line: "Card · MTN MoMo · Airtel Money".

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: browse the storefront (promo cards, then the price in RWF).
2. Key action: checkout, choose MTN Mobile Money, type the phone number.
3. Result: "A payment prompt has been sent to…" then "Order Confirmed".

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a Kigali laptop shop
- Interpretation: fewer scenes and longer holds, soft crossfades and slides, and restrained sound. The confidence comes from showing the real payment flow instead of shouting.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.3s

## Share copy (draft)
Blue-tech: a Rwandan laptop store where prices are in RWF and checkout ends with a MoMo prompt on your phone.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a steady, clean bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean; polished and cinematic tones)
- Music treatment: starts at 0s, volume about 0.28, 0.6s fade-in, fades out over the last 2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`vol-12`, ~110 BPM). Strong cues to target: 8.74s (currency scene starts) and 17.47s (Order Confirmed / logo lands). Beat grid for card arrivals: 4.39, 5.34 (skip, irregular), 6.56, 7.64, using every other beat because these are readable text.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass makes the brand-blue background glow and the active card presence breathe. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, soft
- Audio-coupled moments: promo cards arriving (card sounds), typed phone number (keyboard ticks), cursor click on MoMo (click), Order Confirmed (soft bell)
- Restraint rule: no SFX louder than the music bed's accents, and no stacked hits.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.3s
Black. "Considered objects." sets in word by word (about 0.3s each), then holds fully settled for at least 1.5s. Tagline "Built to last — laptops chosen with care, not just specs." fades up at about 1.3s and holds.
Sequential/interaction: two words appear one after the other.
Audio intent: calm, confident. The bed fades in.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft drop as the headline lands.
Music: steady bed enters.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The shelf — 5.4s (3.3–8.74)
Four promo cards (real titles and brand gradients) arrive one by one in a 2x2 or single row. The site header ("Blue-tech" and a search bar) sits above. Each card shows its real title and subtitle. Copy is read at the floor: each card is visible for 1.1s or more before the next arrives, and the full set holds at the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cards arrive one by one, and the whole set holds about 1.2s at the end.
Audio intent: light and rhythmic
Audio-coupled idea: card-place on each arrival, accenting cards 1 and 4 more than 2 and 3.
Music: bed continues
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3, landing on the 8.74s strong cue

### Scene 3 — Priced in RWF — 3.3s (8.74–12.0)
A product-price card shows `$1,000`. A USD / RWF toggle is clicked by a cursor and the price flips and counts up to `1,471,000 RWF`. Caption: "Prices in RWF."
Sequential/interaction: yes — a simulated click on the toggle.
Audio intent: a small satisfying switch
Audio-coupled idea: a switch click when the cursor presses.
Music: bed continues
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The MoMo prompt — 5.5s (12.0–17.47, Order Confirmed at 16.38s)
The checkout panel. A cursor selects "MTN Mobile Money". The phone number `0788 000 000` types in character by character. The panel switches to "A payment prompt has been sent to 0788 000 000." A phone outline appears and taps Approve. The screen flips to a green check and "Order Confirmed" lands at 16.38s (a beat-grid beat). The logo lands on the 17.47s strong cue.
Sequential/interaction: yes — click, typing, a prompt, approve.
Audio intent: tension into release
Audio-coupled idea: keyboard ticks while typing, a click on Approve, a soft bell on "Order Confirmed".
Music: bed continues
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.8s (17.47–21.3)
The Blue-tech mark and wordmark centered. "Considered objects." beneath it. A quiet line: "Card · MTN MoMo · Airtel Money". Everything holds for at least 2s, then the music fades.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolved and calm
Audio-coupled idea: a soft bell rings over the fading music.
Music: fades out over the last 2s

**Music mood for this video:** steady, clean, corporate-warm
**Audio summary:** a low bed with four card sounds, one switch click, typing ticks, and a single bell on Order Confirmed.
